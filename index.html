About Me

I am a Senior Staff Research Scientist at Google Zurich, where my team and I focus on human understanding and building state-of-the-art egocentric intelligence solutions for XR and beyond.

Previously at Google, I developed novel natural alpha matting and portrait relighting solutions, leveraged synthetic data generated from digital humans, and contributed to core features across Google Meet, Camera, Photos, and Slides. Before joining Google, I was a Researcher at Microsoft Research, where I contributed to the Holoportation project, and earned my PhD from the University of Alicante specializing in real-time 3D reconstruction and depth sensing.
